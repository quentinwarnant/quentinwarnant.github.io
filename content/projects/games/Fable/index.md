### Role: Senior rendering engineer
I embedded for 12 months full-time with the team at Playground Games, adopting their custom engine (ForzaTech), tools and workflows. During which I independently & collaboratively found & optimised CPU & GPU bottlenecks as well as fixed visual bugs under strict timelines leading up to final few milestones (XBOX 2026 Showcase, Gamescom Showcase, Zero Bug Build, Day 0 patch).

#### Contributions:
- CPU & GPU performance optimisation on Gen 9 consoles & PC
- Fixed visual bugs, whilst avoiding content re-authoring 
- Improved tooling
